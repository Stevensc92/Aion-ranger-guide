# Daevanion

This section is brief. It's normal not to be able to complete all four classic Daevanion trees while leveling, and I recommend leaving each tree partially complete.

Your priority during leveling should be the **Unique** nodes (combat speed, cooldown reduction, and similar bonuses) in each tree. Then take the **Legend** nodes (blue) for active skills, prioritizing your main skills, and the **Rare** nodes (green) for passive skills, prioritizing your most important passives.

For the remaining **white (Normal)** nodes, I recommend taking **Attack Bonus**, then **Critical Hit**, then **HP**. After those, you can invest in **Defense Bonus**, followed by **MP** and **Critical Hit Resist**.

!!! info
    Overall, after collecting all Daevanion points available from quests, the Shugo Shop, and Sealed Dungeons, you should still be about **15 points** short of completing all four trees. That's normal; we had the same situation on Taiwan. The nodes you'll be missing mostly provide stats that have little impact on PvE.
